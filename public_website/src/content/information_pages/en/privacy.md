This policy covers the Schublade apps for iPhone and Mac, the Schublade archive you deploy yourself, and this website.

## In short

The Schublade apps collect no data for their developer. There is no account with the developer, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDK in the apps. The developer never receives your documents, your token, or anything about how you use the apps.

## The iPhone and Mac apps

The apps talk only to the archive you connect them to. That archive is your own self-hosted Schublade archive, a Cloudflare Worker in your own Cloudflare account.

- **Documents.** Documents you scan, share, or pick go to your archive and nowhere else.
- **The copy on your device.** The app keeps an offline copy of your archive's records and of the originals you downloaded, so you can read and search without a connection. The Mac app also keeps a small database of the folders you chose and the files it has sent.
- **Your token.** The archive's token is stored in the Keychain of your device. On the iPhone, only the app and its share extension can read it. It leaves your device only in requests to your archive.
- **Camera, Photos, and Files.** The iPhone app uses the camera only for the document scanner. It reads Photos and Files only for the documents you pick.
- **Folders on your Mac.** The Mac app reads only the folders you add, to find documents your archive lacks.
- **Removing the app.** Deleting the app removes its copy, its token, and its settings from the device. Your archive stays as it is.

## Your archive

You deploy the archive on your own Cloudflare account, and it belongs to you. You decide what goes into it and who may sign in. Cloudflare runs it as your provider, under your agreement with Cloudflare. The developer of Schublade has no access to your archive and receives no data from it.

## This website

This website is a set of static pages hosted on Cloudflare. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking, and loads no fonts or scripts from other servers.

To deliver the pages and protect them from attacks, Cloudflare processes technical data of each request, such as your IP address, the page requested, the time, and your browser's user agent. Cloudflare may keep this data in logs for a short time and may process it outside Switzerland and the European Union, including in the United States. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare describes its processing in its [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
