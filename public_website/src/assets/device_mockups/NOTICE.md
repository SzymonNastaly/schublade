# Device mock-ups

These pictures show Schublade's iPhone and Mac applications inside Apple's product bezels from the
[Apple Design Resources](https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/). Apple's license for those
resources covers them, not this repository's AGPL-3.0: they may be used only as mock-ups of software
that runs on Apple's platforms, and the bezels may not be extracted from them. The bezel files themselves
are not in this repository. `public_website/scripts/compose_device_mockups.swift` composes these pictures.
